About Us

Make A Blood Relation brings together blood donation information, donor and receiver forms, and blood availability listings. It is designed to help people learn about donation and connect with others during blood needs.

Please contact the relevant blood bank or healthcare provider to confirm availability and eligibility before donating or requesting blood.
